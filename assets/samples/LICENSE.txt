Instrument samples (piano.json, epiano.json, guitar.json) are note
recordings from the FluidR3_GM SoundFont by Frank Wen, distributed in
mp3 form by the midi-js-soundfonts project (github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts),
MIT license. FluidR3_GM itself is released under the MIT license.

Each JSON maps MIDI note number -> base64 mp3 (C2..C6, every 2 semitones).
